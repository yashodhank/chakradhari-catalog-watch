Raw material comparisons use the stated net metal weight and purity where available. Missing purity, conflicting claims, plating and unknown alloy composition prevent a point estimate. The residual is a non-metal retail premium—not automatically “making charges”—and may include workmanship, stones, design, packaging and margin.
Bullion benchmarks exclude GST and making charges. Monthly base-metal references, when available, are marked with their month and conversion rate; they are not daily Indian retail quotes. Gemstones, Rudraksha and unspecified alloys need product-specific evidence. Retail remainder includes all unexplained costs; tax and making charges cannot be separated without an invoice.
