Use these rates only where a listing states usable net metal weight and purity. Gemstones, Rudraksha and unspecified alloys do not have a universal per-unit benchmark.

What the comparison means
It uses only stated net metal weight and purity. Missing purity, plating, conflicting claims and unknown alloys prevent a point estimate. The remainder is a retail premium, not automatically a making charge.

Bullion benchmarks exclude GST and making charges. Monthly base-metal references, when available, are marked with their month and conversion rate; they are not daily Indian retail quotes. Tax and making charges cannot be separated without an invoice.
